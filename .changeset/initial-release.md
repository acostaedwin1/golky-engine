## "golky-engine": minor

First public release of the engine: Berger-table fixtures (single or double leg, groups with
leftovers), tournament rounds and slots, the full match lifecycle with walkovers, scoring,
standings recomputed from published matches with configurable tie-breakers (incl. head-to-head
and average points), card fines, discipline and scorer rankings, ban-list and roster checks.
